01 Who we are

Data controller and contact

This Privacy Policy explains how Chain Protocol handles your personal data. The data controller is Filemon Rafael de Oliveira, an individual resident in the United Kingdom ("we", "us").

It covers the app and its website and follows the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

For any privacy question or request, write to privacy@chainprotocol.app. This address is also the contact of the person in charge of data processing (encarregado) under the LGPD.

02 Data we collect

What we store and where it comes from

What you give us:

  • Account: e-mail, display name, optional profile photo and how you sign in (e-mail, Apple or Google). With Apple's "Hide My Email" we only see the relay address.
  • Onboarding answers (how you found us, your goal and experience) and your acceptance of the Terms, this policy and the Risk Disclaimer, with date, version and language.
  • Learning: completed lessons, quiz answers and scores, XP, favourites, bookmarks, notes and video progress.
  • Portfolio (subscribers): the wallets, transactions, amounts, prices, fees, tags, notes, goals and targets you type, and the daily values we calculate from them.
  • Preferences: currency and notification settings.

What is collected automatically:

  • Device: push notification token, app version, a random installation ID, language, time zone and the country of your device's region settings (not GPS).
  • Usage and diagnostics: screens viewed and buttons tapped (Firebase Analytics, when enabled) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Purchases: Apple transaction IDs, product, dates and status. We never receive card or bank details.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photo library (only the photo you choose), seed phrases, private keys, exchange logins or identity documents.

03 Why we use it

Purposes and legal bases

  • To provide the Service (account, lessons, progress, notes, Portfolio, purchases): performance of our contract with you (UK/EU GDPR art. 6(1)(b); LGPD art. 7, V).
  • To keep records of your acceptances and of purchases: legal obligations and our legitimate interest in being able to prove them (art. 6(1)(c) and (f); LGPD art. 7, II, VI and IX).
  • To keep the app secure and working, fix crashes and prevent abuse: legitimate interests (art. 6(1)(f); LGPD art. 7, IX).
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it through analytics: legitimate interests; you can object at any time (see "Your choices").
  • To send the notifications you asked for: your consent, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings at any time (art. 6(1)(a); LGPD art. 7, I).

We don't sell or rent your data, use it for advertising or build advertising profiles, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. We make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing with legal or similarly significant effects: the Risk Score and other calculations are educational estimates you are free to ignore.

04 Who we share it with

Service providers

We share data only with providers that process it on our behalf to run the Service, under contracts that protect it:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, file storage and server functions.
  • Google Firebase: Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config and Cloud Messaging (push).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store payments and push notifications.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, if you use it.
  • Resend: account e-mails (codes and confirmations).
  • Mux: streaming of the lesson videos.
  • Cloudflare: our domain and e-mail routing.

Some market data is requested by your device directly from DEX Screener, Binance and DefiLlama. They receive your IP address and the request, as any website would, but no account data. Our server also reads public market data from CoinGecko, alternative.me, Coin Metrics, OKX and Bybit without sending any personal data.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, to protect rights and safety, or to whoever takes over the Service (you would be told in advance).

05 International transfers

Where your data is processed

Our providers may process data outside the UK, the European Economic Area and Brazil, for example in the United States. When they do, we rely on adequacy decisions (including the UK–US Data Bridge and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified providers), the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, and the safeguards allowed by article 33 of the LGPD.

06 How long we keep it

Retention and deletion

  • Account, learning, notes and Portfolio data: while your account exists. Deleting your account in the app removes them from our database at once; copies in our providers' backups are overwritten in their normal cycles.
  • Profile photos: deleted with your account or when you replace them.
  • Purchase and subscription records: kept for up to 6 years for accounting, tax and refunds, no longer linked to you once you delete your account.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months. Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Data kept only on your device (preferences, onboarding answers before sign-in, Face ID sign-in details in the iOS Keychain) stays there until you delete the app or turn the option off.

07 Your rights

Access, correction, deletion and more

Depending on where you live, you can: access your data and get a copy; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to its use (including analytics); receive it in a portable format; withdraw consent; and, under the LGPD, confirm whether we process your data, know who we share it with and ask for unnecessary data to be anonymised or blocked.

You can change your name, e-mail and photo and delete your account directly in the app. For anything else, write to privacy@chainprotocol.app. We reply within one month (UK/EU) or within the LGPD deadlines, and may first need to confirm your identity.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority: the ICO in the UK (ico.org.uk), your local authority in the EU or the ANPD in Brazil (gov.br/anpd). We'd appreciate the chance to help you first.

08 Security

How we protect your data

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Row-level security in the database lets each account read only its own data; profile photos are kept in a private storage bucket.
  • Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and never stored in plain text. Face ID sign-in keeps your credentials only in your device's Keychain.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your data and puts you at risk, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

09 Children

Adults only

Chain Protocol is for people aged 18 or over. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has created an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

10 Your choices

Notifications, analytics and your device

  • Push notifications are sent only if you allow them; turn them off in iOS Settings or in More → Notifications.
  • Analytics record which screens and buttons are used and are linked to your random user ID, never to an advertising ID. The app doesn't use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and doesn't ask for tracking permission because it doesn't track you. To object to analytics, write to us.
  • Face ID / Touch ID checks happen on your device; we never receive biometric data.
  • The app doesn't use cookies, and the website only hosts these documents, without tracking cookies.

11 Changes to this policy

Updates and new versions

We'll update this policy when the app or the law changes. Each version has a number and a date at the top. When a change matters, the app shows it to you before it applies.
